Desktop: drag to orbit, scroll to zoom.
Headset: left stick turns / tilts; right stick up/down zooms and left/right rolls. Hold either grip to move / rotate. Hold both grips to turn it like a ball; move both hands together to reposition. Release to leave it in place. With supported hand tracking, pinch instead of gripping. The C / Z / T panel is also available inside the headset. Choose Pin here to leave it in the room; Follow me makes it follow your head again. To place it elsewhere, choose Follow me, look towards the new location, then Pin here. Pinning lasts for the current VR/AR session. Bring VR controls back restores the panel in front of you. To use the panel: point and hold the trigger to drag a slider (pinch with hand tracking). Z marks an outline in the volume; Slice only shows that plane alone. For time series, hold the left trigger and move the left stick left/right to scroll through time.
